No exploits, executors, injectors, macros, auto-clickers, or mods of any kind. This includes speed, fly, teleport, and noclip. No warning, no appeal.

Rule 01 / 20Fair Play

Found a bug? Open a ticket right away. Using, hiding, sharing, or selling it counts as exploiting.

Rule 02 / 20Fair Play

Scams, fake giveaways, phishing links, begging, and account selling or trading are not allowed. Robux and real-money deals outside official systems are also banned.

Rule 03 / 20Security

Never share your password, cookie, or personal info. Staff will never ask for them. If your account gets compromised, that's on you.

Rule 04 / 20Security

Using an alt to dodge a ban, mute, or kick gets every linked account banned.

Rule 05 / 20Security

No insults, bullying, threats, discrimination, hate speech, doxxing, or targeted harassment, in-game or on Discord. Punishment depends on severity.

Rule 06 / 20Conduct

Don't ruin rounds, troll, block or trap players, or work against your team.

Rule 07 / 20Gameplay

Don't use coded words, symbols, or spacing tricks to get around the chat filter.

Rule 08 / 20Conduct

Don't pretend to be staff, developers, the owner, Night Frame Studios, Roblox, or another player.

Rule 09 / 20Conduct

You must follow the Roblox Terms of Use and Community Standards at all times.

Rule 10 / 20Conduct

Don't argue with staff, mini-mod, or call people out publicly. Open a ticket and bring evidence.

Rule 11 / 20Conduct

Fake reports, fake evidence, and ticket abuse get the same punishment as the offense you reported.

Rule 12 / 20Conduct

Leaking or sharing unreleased content, builds, assets, or private studio info is forbidden.

Rule 13 / 20Studio

Copying, stealing, or redistributing our maps, models, scripts, or UI is forbidden, and so is helping someone else do it.

Rule 14 / 20Studio

Refund abuse, chargebacks, and false payment disputes will get your items removed.

Rule 15 / 20Studio

Throwing matches, boosting accounts, or rigging rankings is forbidden. Everyone involved gets banned.

Rule 16 / 20Fair Play

AFK scripts, teaming up, or any unintended way of farming XP or currency is prohibited. Gains are reset, then repeat offenders are banned.

Rule 17 / 20Fair Play

No spam, excessive caps, or advertising other games, servers, groups, or socials. Mute first, ban for repeat offenses.

Rule 18 / 20Conduct

Don't insult, pressure, or repeatedly ping staff or developers, and don't demand punishments for others.

Rule 19 / 20Conduct

Looking for loopholes, leaving mid-moderation to dodge punishment, or baiting others into breaking rules is forbidden. Staff judge by intent, not just wording.
